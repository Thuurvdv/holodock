# Changelog

## 1.0.1 - 2026-08-01

- Fixed HoloDock activation chat cards on Foundry VTT v12 by resolving the ChatMessage document class through Foundry's configured document APIs before reporting chat as unavailable.

## 1.0.0 - 2026-07-08

- Initial HoloDock release for Foundry VTT v12, v13, and v14.
- HoloDock adds a system-agnostic HoloSuite console for managing ship profiles, loadouts, equipment slots, power allocation, damage state, and ship-image pins in sci-fi or vehicle-focused campaigns.
